# Model Zero — rulings ledger

Rulings of the chief architect (David Clancy) on the definitions of Model Zero, the RP1 checker and the authentication step. A ruling is recorded here when it changes, or constrains a future change to, a definition in this repository. Earlier rulings (D-A to D-O, R16/R17, the Strike resolutions) are recorded in the header of `ModelZero.lean`, in the Problem Book pages under `docs/problem-book/`, and in the Task 2 correspondence note under `checker/why3/`; this ledger carries them forward from D-P. Each entry names the issue that tracks its implementation.

Terms: *Curator* — the party who sets an experience up (the application's `curator_id`). *Hosts* — the parties who deliver it (host-performer, host-producer, host-supplier). *Steward* — the host-supplier role that runs authentication on the day. *Authenticator* — the staked, independent party who signs the delivery attestation. *Y-side* — the supplier half of the Step 1 allocation. *LEAP* — the Live Experience Authentication Protocol; L0–L3 its levels. *RUIPA* — the weekly participation allowance. *R1–R7* — the chain requirements of the identity-inclusion paper (6 August 2026).

| **D-V** | 17 Oct 2026 | Protocol distinct from ledger | The ROCKR protocol (attestation format, checker, entitlement rules) is a public building block any ledger may run; the proofs in this repository are proofs about the protocol, not about any one running ledger — the checker is chain-agnostic. The ROCKRCOIN chain is one ledger running it, kept by the live-experience validator set and carrying the digital utility asset. A state, region or group of states may run a sovereign ledger on the same protocol with validators of its own, carrying attestations and entitlements but never issuing the asset: issuance happens only on the ROCKRCOIN chain. Where a state's welfare design lets a live experience on its instance earn the asset, that instance is, for that experience, an accredited ROCKR app instance (dual-homed: welfare attestations to the sovereign ledger; live-experience economics on the ROCKRCOIN chain as a client); the credit reaches Main by issuance, not transfer. What crosses between ledgers over IBC is an identity linkage and, optionally, a mirrored attestation — no personal data. Trusting what arrives from another ledger is a new, named hypothesis **H6**, declared and unused, stated wherever the public-sector path is described; issuance never depends on it. Vocabulary in outward documents: "the ROCKR protocol", "the ROCKRCOIN chain", "a sovereign ledger"; "the blockchain" retired. | #8 (dedup re-scoped cross-ledger); Problem Book §4 (H6) |
| **D-W** | 22 Oct 2026 | Licence across the estate | Apache 2.0 for Model Zero, its proof artefacts, the RP1 checker and the application, without field-of-use restriction; no source-available or change-date variant. The singleness of the asset rests on two mechanisms, not on the licence: issuance requires an accredited issuer key admitted by on-chain governance on the ROCKRCOIN chain, and ROCKR and ROCKRCOIN are registered marks. The sentence "the licence will ensure any deployment uses ROCKRCOIN" is retired from the project description and whitepaper. Lawyer's check before the board minute is final: enforceability of accreditation as a governance rule; trademark registration status (UK, EU, France); a framework-agreement line permitting ROCKR Preuves to run a sovereign-ledger pilot under the open licence. | Board minute §6.1(b); whitepaper v8.3 |
| **D-X** | 22 Oct 2026 | Validator independence for public-sector ledgers | A published governance constraint on any public-sector ledger running the ROCKR protocol: no single constituency holds a quarter or more of the validator set; at least one validator sits outside the jurisdiction whose entitlements the ledger releases; every validator runs the proved checker unmodified. Default constituencies: the paying agency, the national audit office, the regions or municipalities, the social partners, the identity provider, and one peer from outside the jurisdiction (a peer state, an EU body or an audit institution — never the live-experience chain). Any study begins on a multi-validator testnet (build plan Part 4) with the participating agencies and a university as validators. The programme has not built a second ledger, specified cross-ledger de-duplication, or run a multi-validator testnet; the rule is design. | Build plan Part 4; welfare study |
