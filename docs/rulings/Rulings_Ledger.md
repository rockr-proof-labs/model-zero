# Model Zero — rulings ledger

Rulings of the chief architect (David Clancy) on the definitions of Model Zero, the RP1 checker and the authentication step. A ruling is recorded here when it changes, or constrains a future change to, a definition in this repository. Earlier rulings (D-A to D-O, R16/R17, the Strike resolutions) are recorded in the header of `ModelZero.lean`, in the Problem Book pages under `docs/problem-book/`, and in the Task 2 correspondence note under `checker/why3/`; this ledger carries them forward from D-P. Each entry names the issue that tracks its implementation.

Terms: *Curator* — the party who sets an experience up (the application's `curator_id`). *Hosts* — the parties who deliver it (host-performer, host-producer, host-supplier). *Steward* — the host-supplier role that runs authentication on the day. *Authenticator* — the staked, independent party who signs the delivery attestation. *Y-side* — the supplier half of the Step 1 allocation. *LEAP* — the Live Experience Authentication Protocol; L0–L3 its levels. *RUIPA* — the weekly participation allowance. *R1–R7* — the chain requirements of the identity-inclusion paper (6 August 2026).

(iv) The Y-side is partitioned into lines — Host, Curator, Venue, Production, AI, Authentication; Venue + Production + AI is the service envelope; conservation over the sum.
